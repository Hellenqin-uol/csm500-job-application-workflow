Copy the feed URL and add it as a subscription calendar in your calendar application. The feed is read-only. Changes made in your external calendar will not be written back to Job Tracker.

Outlook Calendar

  1. Open Outlook Calendar.
  2. Select "Add calendar".
  3. Choose "Subscribe from web".
  4. Paste the feed URL and save.

Apple Calendar

  1. Open Apple Calendar.
  2. Select "File" -> "New Calendar Subscription".
  3. Paste the feed URL.
  4. Confirm the subscription settings.

Google Calendar

  1. Open Google Calendar in a browser.
  2. Next to "Other calendars", select "From URL".
  3. Paste the feed URL.
  4. Add the calendar.

Note: Calendar applications may not update subscribed feeds immediately. Refresh intervals depend on the calendar provider and can sometimes be configured on the subscribed calendar.
